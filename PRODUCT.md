# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tradesmen — plumbers, handymen, electricians, and other independent contractors. They work on-site with their hands, often glancing at their phone or laptop between jobs. They're not office workers and don't want to feel like they're using corporate software. Their primary tasks: look up a customer's history before arriving on a job, send an invoice the moment a job is done, and book the next appointment without fuss.

## Product Purpose

A CRM built specifically for tradesmen. Manages customer profiles, jobs, invoicing, and scheduling in one dark-mode tool that feels like it was built for the trade — not adapted from a generic SaaS template. The built-in AI assistant handles the admin so the tradesman can focus on the work. Success means a plumber can go from finished job to paid invoice in under 60 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Reliable. Sharp. Direct.

The tool should feel like a well-made piece of trade equipment — heavy, purposeful, no wasted detail. Dark surfaces, strong type hierarchy, decisive UI. Not trying to impress with flash; impressing with dependability.

## Anti-references

- Bloated enterprise software (Salesforce, SAP) — overcrowded screens, too many fields, nothing clearly primary
- Childish or consumer apps — bright palettes, rounded-everything, playful copy that would embarrass a tradesman showing a client their invoice
- Generic SaaS blue-and-white — bland template feel, zero identity
- Glassmorphism / neon glow aesthetics — decorative trends with no substance

## Design Principles

1. **Job first** — every screen is optimised for the task a tradesman does on that screen. No decoration that competes with the primary action.
2. **Earned density** — pack information intelligently, not recklessly. A tradesman wants facts, not whitespace. But never clutter.
3. **Dark by occupation** — dark surfaces because this is used outdoors in bright sun, in vans, on site. Not dark because it's trendy.
4. **Invoice in 60 seconds** — the invoice creation and send flow is the most important interaction. It must be frictionless above all else.
5. **AI handles the admin** — the AI assistant is a working part of the tool, not a chatbot bolted on. It should be able to complete real tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High-contrast text on dark backgrounds. Touch targets large enough for work-roughened hands. No motion required for core functionality.
